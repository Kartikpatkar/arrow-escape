## 2. Information Collection and Use

### A. Personal Information
**Arrow Escape: Arrow Puzzle does NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contact list, audio recordings, camera feed, or biometric data. You can play the entire game anonymously without creating an account or registering.

### B. Locally Stored Game Data
To provide a seamless gameplay experience, the App stores progress locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and Capacitor storage. This includes:
- Levels completed, star ratings, and high scores.
- Daily Challenge completion history and streaks.
- Consumable items (e.g., hint count).
- Customization preferences (selected board themes, arrow skins, and trail effects).
- User settings (sound toggle, haptics toggle, animation pacing).
- Cryptographic HMAC checksums used solely for offline save integrity verification.

This data remains strictly on your device and is not uploaded to any private external servers.

## 3. Device Permissions & Purpose
The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its intended functionality:
- **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**: Used to generate tactile haptic feedback during arrow movement, collisions, and puzzle completions. Can be toggled on/off at any time in the in-game Settings.
- **`android.permission.INTERNET` & `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Used strictly to connect with Google Play Services for in-app billing transactions and voluntary rewarded ads.
- **`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Used solely to schedule local opt-in reminders for new daily puzzles. No remote marketing push notifications are sent.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Since game progress is stored locally on your device:
- You may delete all stored game data at any time by tapping **"Reset Game Progress"** in the in-game Settings menu.
- Clearing the App's storage via **Android Settings > Apps > Arrow Escape > Clear Data** or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local game saves.
