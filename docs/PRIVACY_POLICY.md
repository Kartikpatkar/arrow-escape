# Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

**Effective Date:** October 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 10, 2026

Thank you for playing **Arrow Escape** ("the Game"), developed by **Kartik Patkar** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
Arrow Escape is an offline-first casual puzzle game designed with player privacy in mind:
- We **do not** require or support user accounts, registration, or logins.
- We **do not** collect personal identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or age).
- We **do not** access or collect your device contacts, photo library, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

---

## 2. Local Device Storage
All gameplay progress (such as unlocked levels, star achievements, best moves count, hint balances, and chosen visual themes) is stored **exclusively locally on your device** (via local key-value storage). 

We do not operate a remote game server or database. If you uninstall the Game or clear app data via device settings, your local progress will be erased. You can also reset all progress directly in the Game’s Settings menu.

---

## 3. Third-Party Advertising & Services
To support ongoing development and offer the game for free, Arrow Escape integrates third-party services:

### 3.1 Google AdMob (Google LLC)
The Game may display voluntary rewarded video advertisements and paced interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as:
- Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID)
- IP address (used for approximate region / language determination)
- Performance and crash diagnostics

For more details on how Google processes and safeguards data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:  
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 3.2 Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC)
If you purchase the optional "Remove Ads" product, transaction processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or financial details. Google Play communicates only the confirmation of your purchase token to unlock the entitlement on your device.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)
Arrow Escape is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that any child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the Game, please contact us so we can investigate and address it promptly.

---

## 5. Security
Because the Game does not collect or transmit personal information to external backend servers, your gameplay data remains safe on your own device. All communication between the app and Google services (AdMob, Play Billing) is secured via industry-standard encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.

---

## 6. Your Rights
Under applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA):
- You have the right to reset your device's advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertisements via your Android device settings:  
  `Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID`.
- You can delete all local app data at any time through the in-game Settings menu or through Android Application Settings.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
